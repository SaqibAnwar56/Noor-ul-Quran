About Noor-ul-Quran

Noor-ul-Quran is a free online Quran platform created by Saqib Anwar for Muslims who want a simple place to read, listen to and reflect upon the Holy Quran. Explore all 114 Surahs and the traditional 30 Paras (Juz), with Arabic text, translations and Quran recitation audio.

Noor-ul-Quran also provides commonly used Islamic resources including Durood Paak, Ayat-ul-Kursi, the Four Quls, the 99 Names of Allah and a digital Tasbih counter.

The goal of Noor-ul-Quran is to provide a calm, accessible online experience for Quran reading and daily remembrance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Noor-ul-Quran?

Noor-ul-Quran is a free online Quran platform for reading Surahs and Paras, listening to Quran recitation and accessing daily Islamic resources such as Ayat-ul-Kursi, Durood Paak, the Four Quls and the 99 Names of Allah.

Can I read the Quran online?

Yes. Noor-ul-Quran provides access to the Quran by Surah and Para (Juz), with Arabic text, translation and recitation audio.

What is Durood Paak?

Durood Paak, also called Durood-e-Ibrahim or Salat al-Ibrahimiyyah, is a salutation upon the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ recited in Salah and on other occasions.

What is Ayat-ul-Kursi?

Ayat-ul-Kursi is verse 255 of Surah Al-Baqarah. It is widely known as the Verse of the Throne and is frequently recited by Muslims.

What are the Four Quls?

The Four Quls commonly refer to Surah Al-Kafirun, Surah Al-Ikhlas, Surah Al-Falaq and Surah An-Nas.

What are the 99 Names of Allah?

The 99 Names of Allah, known as Al-Asma-ul-Husna, are the beautiful names and attributes of Allah commonly studied and remembered by Muslims.

Durood Paak

Durood Paak, also known as Durood-e-Ibrahim or Salat al-Ibrahimiyyah, is a salutation upon the Prophet ﷺ. Read the Arabic text, transliteration and English translation.

Durood Ibrahim

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa 'ala aali Muhammad, kama sallayta 'ala Ibrahima wa 'ala aali Ibrahim, innaka Hameedum Majeed.

اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

Allahumma barik 'ala Muhammadin wa 'ala aali Muhammad, kama barakta 'ala Ibrahima wa 'ala aali Ibrahim, innaka Hameedum Majeed.

O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent blessings upon Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim; indeed You are Praiseworthy, Glorious. O Allah, bless Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You blessed Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim; indeed You are Praiseworthy, Glorious.

Reported in Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim, from the hadith of Ka'b ibn 'Ujrah (may Allah be pleased with him).

Why Muslims recite Durood Paak

The Quran instructs believers to send blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ in Surah Al-Ahzab 33:56. Durood Sharif is commonly recited during Salah, after the Adhan, on Fridays and when the blessed name of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is mentioned.
